Step-By-Step Mechanics

HOW THE INK SEEPS

Transparency isn't just about eco-inks. It means showing you exactly why our prints stay bright and soft for decades.

01

THE ECO-INK SPECS

We source ultra-premium water-based and discharge inks. Unlike toxic plastisol, our pigments bind physically to the organic cotton threads. This leaves a hand-feel that breathes perfectly and doesn't crack, melt, or stick to your body on warm afternoons.

02

THE MANUAL PRESSES

Automatic machinery prints fast but crushes the textile fibres. We utilize traditional, rock-solid heavy-duty manual carousel presses. Every squeegee stroke is carefully measured by our press operators to guarantee perfect opacity, sharp line register, and uniform ink deposit.

03

THE SLOW CURING

Once printed, shirts travel along our industrial forced-air dryer. Sustained, calibrated heat ensures the water evaporate is complete and pigments fuse permanently into the yarn. No peeling, no fast fade. Just tough apparel ready to undergo hundreds of hard washes.
