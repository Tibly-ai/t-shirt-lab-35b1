Heavy Blank Program

High-Density Plastisol

Our ink cures thick. We layer pigment screens repeatedly to build massive raised textures, high-contrast halftones, and rich saturated fields that outlast normal machine washes.

Mesh Tension Control

Every screen is stretched under strict mechanical monitoring in Brooklyn. This results in razor-sharp details, solid borders, and flawless halftone gradients.

PRODUCTION CRITERIA

NO WEAK SPOTS IN THE SETUP.

Every custom screen-print run and seasonal garment capsule passes through a rigorous physical proofing checklist. If a print lacks depth or texture, we clear the screens and burn them again.

01

Vector Optimization Process

We process incoming designs through our specialized halftone algorithm to maximize mechanical edge contrast on screen surfaces.

02

Custom Pigment Lab

No generic buckets of ink. We hand-mix exact matches for your brand specs using heavy premium carrier bases that grip heavy garment weaves.

03

Industrial Heat Cure

Each garment travels through a long infrared conveyor gas dryer to bake plasticizers permanently into cotton threads. No washing out, cracking, or peeling.

04

Label Customization

Complete your capsule drops with custom screen-printed interior sizing labels, neck brand signatures, and custom heavy-cardboard hang tags.
